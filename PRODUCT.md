# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are patients and their families in and around Nganjuk who are looking for hospital information, available doctors and schedules, services, and a way to register for care online.

## Product Purpose

RS Bhayangkara Nganjuk’s website helps patients quickly understand the hospital’s services, find relevant doctors and schedules, access practical information, and complete online registration. Success means patients can take the next step toward care with confidence and minimal friction.

## Positioning

The product promise is excellent and fast service for patients, expressed through clear information access and efficient digital pathways to registration and care planning.

## Operating Context

Patients and family members may use the site from phones or desktop devices while deciding where to seek care, checking a doctor’s availability, preparing for a visit, or completing registration. Hospital staff use a separate administrative interface to manage public content, schedules, doctors, services, FAQs, partners, testimonials, and registration records.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The web application provides public pages for hospital information, services, facilities, doctors, doctor schedules, news, FAQs, officials, testimonials, partners, login, and online registration, including QR-based registration flows. It also contains authenticated administrative workflows for managing public content and registrations. The existing implementation uses Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript/JavaScript, Prisma-backed data access, and a component library based on shadcn/Radix-style primitives.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current public interface, administrative interface, hospital logo and image assets, structured service/doctor/schedule/news data modules, and live page copy. No additional testimonials, benchmarks, accreditation details, legal claims, or external brand constraints were provided during init; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next patient action obvious.
- Keep essential care information fast to find and understand.
- Reduce friction in scheduling and registration workflows.
- Communicate with clarity and respect for patients and families.
- Preserve operational accuracy over decorative invention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard or user need was established during init. Future work should preserve accessible web fundamentals and avoid introducing barriers to patients using mobile devices, assistive technologies, or slower connections.
